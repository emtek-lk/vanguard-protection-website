A fire extinguisher is only useful if it works on the day you need it. Routine checks take minutes and catch most problems early.

## A simple monthly walk-around

- **Location:** the extinguisher is in its designated place, visible and not blocked by stock or furniture.
- **Access:** staff can reach it quickly, and the signage is clear.
- **Condition:** no dents, corrosion or damage to the hose, nozzle or handle.
- **Pressure gauge:** the needle is in the operating (green) zone.
- **Safety pin and seal:** both are intact.

## Know the right type

Different fires need different extinguishers. Make sure the types you hold match the risks on your premises, such as electrical equipment, kitchens or flammable liquids.

## When to call a professional

Scheduled servicing, refilling after use, and any unit that fails a visual check should be handled by a qualified provider. Training your team to use an extinguisher confidently is just as important as the equipment itself.
