Many buildings still run on a "fix it when it breaks" basis. It feels cheaper until a pump fails in the middle of the week or a generator does not start during an outage.

## The planned maintenance cycle

A structured programme links every asset to a clear routine:

1. **Asset:** list and tag the equipment you rely on.
2. **Requirement:** decide what each asset needs to stay reliable.
3. **Frequency:** set how often each task should happen.
4. **Task and inspection:** carry out and check the work.
5. **Record and review:** log what was done, then use the history to improve.

## What you gain

- Fewer unexpected failures and less disruption
- Longer asset life and better lifecycle planning
- A clear maintenance history for audits and decisions

The goal is not just to maintain a building, but to help it perform better.
