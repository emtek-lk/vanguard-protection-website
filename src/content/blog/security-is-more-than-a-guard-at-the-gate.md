Placing a security officer at the entrance is a start, not a strategy. Good security begins by understanding the environment and the risks that matter to your site.

## What a risk-based review covers

- Physical security and perimeter
- Access control and visitor management
- CCTV coverage and alarm systems
- Guard deployment and security procedures
- Emergency response and incident reporting
- Key control and known vulnerable areas

## People, process and technology

The strongest setups make these three work together: trained personnel who know the procedures, clear processes for access and incidents, and technology that supports them rather than replaces them.

If you are not sure where your gaps are, a consultancy assessment is a practical first step.
